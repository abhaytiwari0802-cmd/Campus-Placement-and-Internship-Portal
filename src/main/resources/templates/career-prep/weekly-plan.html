8-Week Comprehensive Campus Placement Roadmap

A methodical week-by-week timetable designed for MCA master's students to prepare for high-package campus recruitment

Career Hub
Weeks 1 & 2 Aptitude & Core Java
Foundations & Screening Preparation
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Time & work, percentages, profit & loss, trains, and pipes & cisterns.
  • Logical Reasoning: Syllogisms, blood relations, seating arrangements, and coding-decoding.
  • Core Java Fundamentals: OOP concepts, method overloading vs overriding, memory model (Heap/Stack), and exception handling.
  • Milestone: Complete 2 full-length online aptitude mock assessments under timed conditions.
Weeks 3 & 4 Linear & Non-Linear DSA
Data Structures & Algorithmic Patterns
  • Arrays & Strings: Two pointers, sliding window, prefix sums, and kadane's algorithm.
  • Linked Lists & Stacks/Queues: Reversal, cycle detection, monotonic stacks, and parentheses validation.
  • Trees & BST: Inorder, preorder, postorder traversals, height of tree, and LCA.
  • Milestone: Solve at least 30 targeted medium problems on LeetCode/HackerRank.
Weeks 5 & 6 Database & Enterprise Stack
DBMS, Spring Boot & System Architecture
  • SQL & DBMS: Normalization (1NF to BCNF), ACID transactions, B-Tree indexing, joins, and window functions.
  • Spring Boot Architecture: Dependency injection, REST controllers, Spring Data JPA, Hibernate caching, and Spring Security.
  • Academic Project Review: Document architecture, ER diagrams, data flow diagrams, and deploy code to GitHub with clear README.
  • Milestone: Conduct a simulated code review and architecture walkthrough of your MCA major/minor project.
Weeks 7 & 8 Final Polish & Conversion
Mock Interviews, Resume & HR Readiness
  • Resume Optimization: Format 1-page clean ATS-compliant PDF resume highlighting quantifiable project achievements.
  • Behavioral Prep: Craft STAR responses for leadership, conflicts, failure handling, and strengths/weaknesses.
  • Mock Interviews: Conduct 2 technical peer mock interviews and 1 behavioral mock session with faculty/alumni.
  • Milestone: Upload polished resume to the Campus Placement Portal and apply to eligible campus postings.
