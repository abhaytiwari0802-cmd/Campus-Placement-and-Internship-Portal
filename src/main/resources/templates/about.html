About Our Training & Placement Cell

The Training and Placement Office (TPO) acts as a pivotal bridge connecting aspiring postgraduate and undergraduate engineers with leading technology innovators across the globe.

Our platform automates the end-to-end placement journey: from company registration and credential verification, through eligibility-screened job applications, resume management, interview coordination, and administrative reporting.
