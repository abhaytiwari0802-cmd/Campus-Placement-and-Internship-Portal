How The Placement Portal Works

Streamlined recruitment lifecycle with strict server-side validation

1. Candidate Profile & Resume

2. Job Match & Applications

Intelligent eligibility checks verify CGPA and course prerequisites before candidates submit single or multiple job applications.

3. Interviews & Selection
